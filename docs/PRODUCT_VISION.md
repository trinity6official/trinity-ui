# Product Vision

## Purpose

Trinity6 should help people understand and accomplish complex digital work through an interactive visual environment and Trinity intelligence.

The experience follows five verbs:

**Explore → Learn → Build → Create → Run**

### Explore

Navigate meaningful systems and relationships visually: people, devices, networks, servers, cloud services, applications, databases, controls, and business processes.

### Learn

Use interactive explanations, guided scenarios, simulations, challenges, and lessons. Initial domains are cybersecurity, networking, cloud, AI, software, data, privacy, GRC, and IT infrastructure.

### Build

Help users turn goals into real projects. A future user may start an AI business, app, creator workflow, or other digital project and progress through validation, product, brand, launch, marketing, sales, and operations.

### Create

Allow educators and creators to author interactive experiences without programming. Creator experiences should be publishable, shareable, embeddable, and eventually monetizable.

### Run

For serious users and organizations, Trinity can evolve from guidance into an operational assistant using explicit permissions, integrations, workflows, analytics, and automation.

## Initial audience

The first release targets curious learners and technology practitioners. It must also be compelling enough for creators to share. We will not attempt to serve every industry at launch.

## Initial experience

The first world is one company building. Meaningful entities include employee/laptop, Wi-Fi/network device, firewall, server, database, cloud connection, camera/physical access, and application/data flows.

The first flagship guided experience is planned as **“How a company gets hacked — and how to protect it.”**

## Trinity

Trinity is the intelligence layer. It should not be designed as merely a floating chat widget. Over time Trinity can answer questions, focus/highlight entities, explain relationships, run guided experiences, generate starting worlds, and help users execute projects.

The AI implementation remains independently owned by `trinity-ai`.

## Business model hypothesis

Start with a useful free experience to maximize learning, sharing, and creator adoption. Paid capability should unlock increased usage and professional value rather than making the free product intentionally poor. Potential future value includes advanced creation, private projects, collaboration, analytics, automation, integrations, premium templates/assets, and organizational capabilities.

Pricing and tier limits are intentionally not fixed until usage and cost data exist.

## What we are not building yet

- an open-ended metaverse;
- avatars and walking mechanics;
- a generic 3D game;
- a marketplace before creators exist;
- enterprise digital twins before the public interaction model is validated;
- arbitrary industry worlds without demonstrated demand.
